Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

This policy covers every Reverence Media game: Clay Wheel, Paint Mixer, Sand Art Jars, Sand Slice, Paint Pour and Groove, on iPhone and Android.

The short version

Our games don't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you do in a game is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Each game saves a few things on your phone so it can pick up where you left off: settings such as sound on or off, and your creations, such as the pots on your Clay Wheel shelf or your current painting in Paint Mixer. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the game deletes it.

Camera and photos (Paint Mixer)

Paint Mixer can use your camera, only if you allow it, so you can pick a color from something around you. The image is used on your device to read the color and is not saved or sent anywhere. When you tap Save, Paint Mixer opens your phone's share sheet so you can save your painting to your photo library or send it with another app. It only does this when you choose to.

Network use

The games work fully offline. They don't contact our servers or any third-party service.

Children

Because our games collect no personal information from anyone, they collect none from children.

Third parties

We don't sell or share data with anyone, because we don't have any. Apple and Google may collect information about app downloads and crashes under their own privacy policies, depending on your device settings.

Changes

If a future version of a game changes how it handles data, we'll update this page and the date above before that version is released.
