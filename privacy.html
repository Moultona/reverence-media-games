Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

This policy covers every Reverence Media game: Clay Wheel, Paint Mixer, Sand Art Jars, Sand Slice, Paint Pour and Groove, on iPhone and Android.

The short version

Our games don't ask for or collect personal information like your name, email or location. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you make in a game stays on your device. There are two exceptions: buying an upgrade, such as Paint Mixer Studio, a Sand Art Jars jar pack or a Groove pack, which goes through Apple or Google; and Groove's world leaderboards, which use Game Center or Google Play Games if you're signed in. The details below explain what each involves.

What stays on your device

Each game saves a few things on your phone so it can pick up where you left off: settings such as sound on or off, and your creations, such as the pots on your Clay Wheel shelf or your current painting in Paint Mixer. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the game deletes it.

Camera and photos (Paint Mixer)

Paint Mixer can use your camera, only if you allow it, so you can pick a color from something around you. The image is used on your device to read the color and is not saved or sent anywhere. When you tap Save, Paint Mixer opens your phone's share sheet so you can save your painting to your photo library or send it with another app. It only does this when you choose to.

In-app purchases (Paint Mixer Studio, Sand Art Jars jar packs, Groove packs)

If you buy an upgrade, payment is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat, a purchase-management service, to confirm your purchase and unlock it, including on a new device when you tap Restore Purchases. For this, RevenueCat receives a random anonymous ID created by the app, your purchase receipt (product, price, currency, date), and basic technical details such as app version, operating system and store country. It does not receive your name, email or anything you make in the game. RevenueCat's own privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

World leaderboards (Groove)

Groove can post your best scores to world leaderboards: one for each place, plus the Daily Challenge. It uses Apple's Game Center on iPhone and Google Play Games on Android. This only happens if you're signed in to Game Center or Play Games on your device; if you're not, you can choose to sign in from the results screen, and the rest of the game works without it. When you are signed in, your score and your Game Center or Play Games player profile (your nickname and picture) are sent to Apple or Google and shown on the leaderboard to other players. We don't receive your Apple or Google account details. You can turn off Game Center in your iPhone's Settings, change your Play Games profile or its visibility in the Play Games app, and remove your scores or profile through Apple or Google. Apple's privacy policy is at apple.com/legal/privacy and Google's at policies.google.com/privacy. The names on Groove's on-device "Top 10" list stay on your phone.

Network use

The games work offline. Apart from the purchase checks and Groove's leaderboards described above, they don't contact our servers or any third-party service.

Children

Our games don't collect personal information from anyone, including children. Groove's world leaderboards use the player's Game Center or Play Games profile, which Apple and Google manage, including their parental controls.

Third parties

We don't sell your data or share it for advertising. Apart from RevenueCat handling purchases and Apple or Google running Groove's leaderboards as described above, no third party receives data from our games. Apple and Google may collect information about app downloads and crashes under their own privacy policies, depending on your device settings.

Changes

If a future version of a game changes how it handles data, we'll update this page and the date above before that version is released.
